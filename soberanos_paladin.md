$$F(\rho, \sigma) = \left( \operatorname{Tr} \sqrt{\sqrt{\rho} \, \sigma \, \sqrt{\rho}} \right)^2 = \|\sqrt{\rho}\sqrt{\sigma}\|_1^2$$

$$d_B(\rho, \sigma) = \sqrt{2 - 2\sqrt{F(\rho, \sigma)}} \le \sqrt{2 - 2\sqrt{0.95}} \approx 0.2250385057$$

$$\Lambda_p(\rho) = (1 - p)\rho + p \frac{\mathbf{I}_n}{n}, \quad \text{con } p = \frac{n \lambda}{1 + n \lambda}, \quad \lambda = \max(0, 10^{-12} - \lambda_{\min}(\rho))$$
